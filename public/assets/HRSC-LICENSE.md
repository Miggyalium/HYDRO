# HRSC data license and attribution

DLR's HRSC site terms apply CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO to downloadable HRSC terrain models. The MC13E download page identifies HMC_13E10_da5.tif as an areoid-referenced terrain model. The three adaptations below may be published under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO.

[DLR HRSC terms](https://hrscteam.dlr.de/imprint_hrsc_en.php) · [MC13E source product](https://hrscteam.dlr.de/public/data/HMC30/quads.php?quad=mc13e) · [CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/igo/)

**Display and download credit:** ESA/DLR/FU Berlin, CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO. Source product: Mars Express HRSC HMC_13E10_da5.tif.

| Packaged asset | Change to disclose |
|---|---|
| relief/hrsc-elevation.tif | Cropped to the Jezero overview and reprojected to the project display grid. |
| relief/hrsc-hillshade.png | Derived with artificial illumination from HRSC terrain, reprojected and cropped to the Jezero overview. |
| contours/hrsc-contours-50m.geojson | Derived 50 m contours with 100 m index lines for the Jezero overview. |

Keep the source, visible credit, license link and relevant change notice with the displayed and downloadable data. Preserve source notices and use the same license for the adaptations. Keep the assets under their data license and avoid implying endorsement. Deployment verification remains pending.
